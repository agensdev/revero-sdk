Revero connects to Claude as a custom connector. Custom connectors are
available on the Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans. The Free plan allows
one.

You need the server address:

```text
https://api.revero.no/mcp
```

<Note>
  Your Revero organisation must have agent access. If [Connected
  agents](https://revero.no/settings/agents) says it is not part of your organisation's
  subscription, contact Revero at [support@revero.no](mailto:support@revero.no) first.
</Note>

## Pro, Max and Free

1. In Claude, open **Customize**, then **Connectors**.
2. Select **Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name the connector `Revero` and paste the server address as the remote MCP
   server URL. Leave the OAuth client settings empty: Claude registers itself
   with Revero.
4. Select **Add**.
5. Select **Connect** on the Revero connector. A Revero sign-in page opens.
6. Sign in with your Revero account. On the consent page, choose the
   organisation and what Claude may do there, then approve.

## Team and Enterprise

An owner of your Claude organisation adds the connector once:

1. Open **Organization settings**, then **Connectors**.
2. Select **Add**, then **Custom**, then **Web**.
3. Name the connector `Revero`, paste the server address, and add it.

Each member then connects it for themselves:

1. Open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, and find Revero.
2. Select **Connect**, sign in with your Revero account, and approve on the
   consent page.

Adding the connector reaches nobody's meetings. Each person's own approval on
the Revero consent page does that, for that person only.

## Use it in a chat

Select **+** at the lower left of the message box, open **Connectors**, and
turn Revero on for the conversation. Then ask, for example, "summarise the
decisions from my meetings this week".

## Remove it

- In Revero, open [Connected agents](https://revero.no/settings/agents) and
  select **Remove access**. This is what withdraws the access.
- In Claude, open **Customize**, then **Connectors**, and remove Revero.

See [what you agree to](/use-revero/overview#what-you-agree-to) for what each
permission on the consent page means.
